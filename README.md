# SALES-PERFORMANCE-DASHBOARD
SALES PERFORMANCE  DASHBOARD USING SQL AND POWERBI
📊 Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

Analyze • Track • Grow

📌 Project Overview

The Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using SQL and Microsoft Power BI.

The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, product trends, customer segments, regional performance, and salesperson activities through interactive visualizations and data-driven insights.

The dashboard consists of three dedicated analytical pages designed to provide a comprehensive view of business performance.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Identify regional sales trends.
- Monitor salesperson performance.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

---

🖥️ Dashboard Pages

1. Sales Overview

Provides a high-level overview of overall business performance.

Key Metrics & Insights:

- Net Sales
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Total Customers
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Sales by Category
- Customer Segmentation
- Top 5 Products
- Sales by Region

2. Product & Customer Analysis

Focuses on product performance and customer-related insights.

Key Metrics & Insights:

- Total Products
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders
- Sales by Category
- Profit by State
- Monthly Quantity Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Sales by Customer Segment

3. Salesperson Analysis

Provides insights into sales performance, order status, and regional distribution.

Key Metrics & Insights:

- Complete Sales
- Total Profit
- Cancelled Orders
- Average Order Value
- Order Status Analysis
- Quantity by City
- Monthly Cost Analysis
- Orders by Region
- Quantity by Category
